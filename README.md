# Supermarket Sales Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes supermarket sales data to identify key business trends, customer behavior patterns, and product performance. The goal is to generate actionable insights that can support decision-making in sales strategy, marketing, and inventory management.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance and revenue trends  
- Identify best-performing product categories  
- Understand customer purchasing behavior  
- Detect peak sales periods  
- Provide data-driven business recommendations  

## Dataset
The dataset contains supermarket transaction records including:
- Invoice details  
- Product categories  
- Sales and quantity  
- Customer type  
- Date and time of purchase  
- Branch and payment method  


## Tools & Technologies
- Python (Pandas, NumPy) – Data cleaning and analysis  
- Matplotlib / Seaborn – Data visualization  
- Jupyter Notebook – Analysis workflow  

## Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Handled missing values  
- Converted date and time formats  
- Removed duplicate records  
- Standardized column names  
- Ensured consistency for analysis  

## Key Insights
- Sales peak during late afternoon and evening hours  
- Certain product categories generate consistently higher revenue  
- Customer type influences spending behavior and basket size  
- Weekend sales are significantly higher than weekdays  
- Payment methods show clear customer preference patterns  

## Business Recommendations
- Increase staffing and inventory during peak hours  
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing product categories  
- Introduce promotions for underperforming segments  
- Optimize stock planning based on weekend demand  
- Strengthen customer retention strategies for high-value customers
